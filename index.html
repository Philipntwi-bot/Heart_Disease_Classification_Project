ℹ️ About This Machine Learning System

This application uses a Random Forest Classifier trained on a synthetic hospital dataset of 1,000 patient records to predict heart disease risk.


Risk Factors Analysed:

  • Age & Sex
  • Blood Pressure & Cholesterol
  • BMI (Body Mass Index)
  • Smoking Status & Diabetes
  • Physical Activity Level
  • Family History of Heart Disease
  • Stress Level
